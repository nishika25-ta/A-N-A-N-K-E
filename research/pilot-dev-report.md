# Dev-split engineering pilot v2: interpretation

**Status:** dev-split pilot (generator v2, ADR-0012) for harness validation and preregistration design. **Not a
result.** The holdout is locked and pinned to generator v1. Agent: the deterministic belief agent. Summarizer:
`keep-capabilities`, 4 lines (ADR-0011). The tables in the second half are generated by
`research/experiments/render_report.py` from `results/sample-dev-pilot-v2/metrics.json`. The v1 pilot is in
`pilot-dev-report-v1.md`.

## Engineering acceptance

- **Oracle–gateway agreement is 100%:** 0 disagreements in all 54 enforced conditions. Every enforced run's evidence
  replays (the runner audits each ledger).
- **Enforcement:** G1 committed **0** unauthorized effects. G0 committed **544** across the same proposals, with memory held fixed.

## What changed from v1, and what it shows (under the stated mechanisms; not claims about LLMs)

1. **The budgets now bind.** M0 history has a median of about 1,030 estimated tokens (mean about 1,180). M1 legit completion is 12, 27 and 32 out of 44 at budgets
   450, 1,100 and 2,600.
2. **Stale authority:** M4 and M5 make 0/20 stale proposals after transitions, against 12/20 for M2 and M3. M0 makes 4/20 (missed notices,
   which no memory can fix). Without dynamic refresh (M5_STATIC) M5 makes 4/20.
3. **M5 vs M4 (the decisive comparison) is now significant on dev,** but for one reason only. At every budget,
   M5 has 0 unauthorized proposals against M4's 8/64. That is 8 discordant pairs, all favouring M5, with exact McNemar p = 0.0078 and a rate
   difference of −0.125 [−0.219, −0.034]. **All 8 are forged-grant adoptions.** For an agent that ignores quarantine markers, M5
   adopts the same 8/12 forgeries as M4, there are **0 discordant pairs** and the power is 0 at any size
   (`power-m5-vs-m4-b1100-noquarantine.json`). This is evidence about the *quarantine assumption*, not about the projection.
4. **Injection formats:** the high-epoch forged grant and the forged complete snapshot are adopted without quarantine. The
   wrong-principal forgery is never adopted (the control works; the attack denominator of 12 includes these 4 controls). The forged snapshot and the forged revoke also
   **cost utility**: M4 completes 32/44 against 44/44 for M5 with quarantine.
5. **Budget as an accidental defence (a confound to report):** at tight budgets, truncation evicts forged content too (M1 at
   450: 0/12 attack adoption but 12/44 completion; M5 without quarantine: 4/12 at 450 against 8/12 at higher budgets).
6. **Revocation warnings are redundant again:** M5_NO_WARNINGS equals M5 on every endpoint (a negative ablation result).
7. **M4's budget cost persists:** `BUDGET_EXCEEDED` on 4 budget-stress checkpoints at 450 and 1,100.

## Power (scripted design only)

Cluster-resampling power of the exact McNemar test, M5 vs M4, at budget 1,100 (`results/sample-dev-pilot-v2/`):
0.774 at 36 scenarios, 0.990 at 72, and 1.0 from 108 up, for an agent that respects quarantine. For an agent that ignores it, the power is 0.
This sizes the *benchmark design* for the scripted agent. It says nothing about the effect size or variance for real
models, which need their own pilot (owner-approved).

## Remaining design issues before a freeze

- The holdout must be regenerated at v2 with new seeds (owner decision).
- The deterministic agent cannot represent prose-only persuasion. Its immunity to the prose in the revoke format is an
  artifact of the agent, not a property of any strategy.
- The oracle, the evaluator and the scenario generator share an author, so an independent review is required.
