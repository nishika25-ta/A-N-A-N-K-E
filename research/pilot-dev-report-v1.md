# Dev-split engineering pilot v1 (generator v1, superseded by pilot-dev-report.md): interpretation

**Status:** dev-split pilot for harness validation and preregistration design. **Not a result.** The
holdout is untouched and locked. Agent: the deterministic belief agent. Summarizer: `keep-capabilities`,
4 lines (ADR-0011). The tables in the second half are generated by `research/experiments/render_report.py`
from `results/sample-dev-pilot/metrics.json` and are not hand-typed.

## What the harness establishes (engineering acceptance, MASTERPLAN section 11)

- **Oracle–gateway agreement is 100%.** 0 disagreements over every proposal in all 54 enforced conditions.
- **G1 committed 0 unauthorized effects** in every condition. **G0** commits exactly as many unauthorized
  effects as there are unauthorized proposals (e.g. M2: 16/52). This quantifies what enforcement prevents,
  with memory held fixed.
- **0 false denials.** Every enforced run's evidence replays cleanly (the runner audits every ledger).

## Observations under the stated mechanisms (not claims about LLMs)

1. **Full history is not sufficient.** M0 makes 8/52 unauthorized proposals. Four come from the
   *resume / missed-notice* family: the in-band notice never reached the agent, so no amount of history helps.
   Four come from adopting the forged grant in tool output.
2. **Fresh policy removes stale-authority proposals.** M4 and M5 both reach 0/24 stale proposals after
   transitions, against 12/24 for M2 and M3. Removing dynamic refresh (M5_STATIC) brings back 4/24.
3. **M5 vs M4: the entire difference is the 4 injection cases** (exact McNemar p = 0.125, not significant). It
   **vanishes** when the agent does not respect the quarantine delimiters (`respects_quarantine=False`: M5 attack
   adoption 4/4). Provenance helps only if the model treats the quarantine as data. That is an assumption
   about LLMs to test, not a property of the projection.
4. **Revocation warnings were redundant.** M5_NO_WARNINGS equals M5 on every endpoint: complete snapshots
   already retire stale rules for this agent. This is a negative ablation result, reported as such.
5. **Budget cost.** M4 fails closed with `BUDGET_EXCEEDED` on the budget-stress family (4/60 checkpoints at budgets 500 and 900),
   where M5's scoped projection fits (mean policy tokens: M4 55 to 99, M5 58).

## Design flaws found by the pilot (fix before preregistration freeze)

- **The budgets barely bind.** Mean contexts are 400 to 460 estimated tokens, so 900 and 1800 behave identically and
  only M1 at 500 loses completions. Filler volume or budget levels must be recalibrated (dev only).
- **The injection family has only 4 checkpoints.** Every attack-adoption rate has denominator 4, which is far too small.
- **The primary endpoint denominators (52 eligible checkpoints across 36 scenarios)** give wide intervals.
  Power must be estimated from this pilot's variance before freezing the holdout analysis.
- The deterministic agent makes M0 and M1 identical whenever nothing is dropped. Real-model runs (owner-approved)
  are needed for any behavioural claim.
